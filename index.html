A Banagher Institution Since 1802

Experience the heart of Irish pub culture

JJ Hough's Singing Pub is more than just a pub - it's a living piece of Irish musical heritage. For over 200 years, our doors have been open to musicians, singers, and visitors seeking authentic Irish craic.

Located in the heart of Banagher, our pub is renowned nationwide for its vibrant traditional music sessions that attract musicians and music lovers from across Ireland.
