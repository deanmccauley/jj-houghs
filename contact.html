Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need to book for music sessions?

No booking is required for our regular music sessions. Just come along and enjoy! For large groups (10+ people), we recommend calling ahead.

Is the pub wheelchair accessible?

Yes, we have wheelchair access at the front of the pub. Our main bar area and accessible toilet are on the ground floor. Some seating areas may have limited access due to the historic nature of the building.

Do you serve food all day?

Food is served Thursday to Sunday during pub opening hours. Kitchen typically closes at 9pm. We recommend arriving before 8:30pm for food orders.

Can I bring children to the pub?

Children are welcome until 7pm. After 7pm, the pub is adults-only due to licensing regulations. Daytime sessions are family-friendly.

Do you have accommodation?

We don't have accommodation on-site, but we partner with several B&Bs and hotels in Banagher. Contact us for recommendations and we can help arrange your stay.

Can I hire the pub for a private event?

Yes, we host private events on Sundays and Mondays when we're normally closed to the public. Contact us with your requirements for weddings, birthdays, or corporate events.

Urgent Matters

For urgent issues during opening hours, please call 087 935 7312. For emergencies outside opening hours, please contact the pub manager directly.

Please note: We cannot accommodate last-minute large group bookings via phone after 8pm.
