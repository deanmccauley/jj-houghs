Musician FAQs

Answers for visiting musicians

Can I bring my instrument and join a session?

Absolutely! All musicians are welcome at JJ Hough's. Just introduce yourself to the session leader when you arrive. We have house instruments available if you're traveling light.

What time should I arrive for sessions?

Sessions typically start around the times listed, but musicians often arrive 30-60 minutes early to settle in. Friday and Saturday sessions are our busiest - arrive early to secure a good spot.

Do you have backline equipment?

We have a basic PA system, microphones, and stands available for scheduled performances. For session playing, we're primarily acoustic - but we can accommodate small amplifiers if needed.

Are there any rules for the sessions?

Respect is our only rule. Wait your turn, tune quietly, and be mindful of volume levels. Our sessions are inclusive - all levels of experience are welcome.
