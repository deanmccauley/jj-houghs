Our Story

Two centuries of music, stories, and authentic Irish craic

Since 1802

A living piece of Irish musical heritage

1802

The Beginning

JJ Hough's first opens its doors as a coaching inn and public house on Main Street, Banagher. Originally serving travelers along the River Shannon, the pub quickly becomes a hub for local farmers, musicians, and storytellers.

1920s

The Music Begins

Spontaneous music sessions become a regular occurrence. Local musicians gather after fairs and markets, establishing JJ Hough's reputation as a place where music lives in the walls.

1970s

National Recognition

Word spreads beyond Offaly as traveling musicians discover the pub's exceptional sessions. JJ Hough's becomes a must-visit destination on Ireland's traditional music circuit.

Today

A Singing Pub Legend

Now renowned nationwide, JJ Hough's continues its tradition of nightly sessions while welcoming a new generation of musicians and music lovers from around the world.

The Singing Pub Tradition

What makes a true Irish singing pub

Community

A singing pub isn't about performers on a stage - it's about everyone participating. From seasoned musicians to first-time singers, all are welcome to join the session.

Spontaneity

No set lists, no rehearsals. The music flows naturally as musicians take turns leading tunes and songs passed down through generations.

Continuity

For over 200 years, the music has never stopped. Young musicians learn from elders, ensuring traditional tunes survive and evolve.

Passion

It's not a job for our musicians - it's a way of life. The passion for traditional music is what makes our sessions truly special.

The JJ Hough's Atmosphere

Step through our doors and you're not just entering a pub - you're becoming part of a living tradition. The warmth of the fire, the glow of the lamps, and the sound of fiddle, flute, and bodhrán create an atmosphere found nowhere else.

Open Fire

Our original fireplace has warmed generations of musicians and visitors.

Traditional Seating

Cosy snugs and wooden benches where conversations and music flow freely.

Proper Pints

Perfectly poured Guinness and a selection of local craft beers and whiskeys.
